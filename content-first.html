Matvey Chakryan

I am Izergil, born Matvey Chakryan, a seeker of beauty in its fading breath. From the decadence of the 19th century and the trembling glow of urban night, I shape visions where phonk’s broken tape meets the poetry of ruin. My art is a bouquet of shadows— flowers bruised, yet luminous— offered to those who hear music in the depths of melancholy. If you seek a simple image, look elsewhere. If you seek atmosphere, memory, and darkness made beautiful, enter the world of Izergil.
